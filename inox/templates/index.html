Need Help?

Questions about Hoses, Pipes & Fittings

Talk to an Engineer

Which stainless steel grades do you offer?

We manufacture in AISI 304, 316, 316L, and 321. Material selection depends on media, temperature, and corrosion environment—our team can recommend the right grade for your application.

What pressure and temperature ranges can your hoses handle?

Our stainless steel corrugated hoses are designed for high pressure service and wide temperatures from approx. −270°C to 700°C (grade & braid dependent). We’ll size the hose and braid to your exact duty conditions.

Do you provide custom assemblies and end connections?

Yes. We supply cut-to-length hoses with flanged or threaded ends, unions, quick-connects, and custom metallic fittings. Options include single/double SS wire braiding, interlock, and pump connectors.

What standards and tests do your products meet?

Products are manufactured and tested as per ISO 10380 and BS 6501. Typical tests include bend, flame, hydrostatic, and burst tests—performed in-house for every production batch.

Do you supply bellows/expansion joints and pump connectors?

Absolutely. We design expansion bellows/joints and flexible pump connectors to compensate for thermal movement, misalignment, vibration, and noise in piping systems.

How do I get a quote and lead time?

Share your media, temperature, pressure, size, length, and end-connection details via our Enquiry form. We’ll respond with a specification sheet, pricing, and delivery schedule.
