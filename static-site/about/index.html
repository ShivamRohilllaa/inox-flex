Welcome

History of Our Company

At InoxFlex, we take pride in being one of India’s leading manufacturers and exporters of Stainless Steel Corrugated Hoses, Hose Assemblies, Metallic Bellows, Expansion Joints, Interlock Hoses, Flexible Pump Connectors, and Pipe Fittings. With years of industry expertise, we specialize in designing and manufacturing hoses from ¼” ID to 12” ID in AISI 304, 316, 316L, and 321 grades, complete with custom mounting fittings and end connections built to match exact client specifications. Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility is located in Bahadurgarh, Haryana (near Delhi) — equipped with modern machinery, advanced welding stations, and comprehensive hydrostatic and pneumatic leak detection testing setups. Every product undergoes strict quality checks in compliance with ISO and BS standards, ensuring exceptional performance, durability, and safety under high-pressure and high-temperature conditions. We serve a wide range of industries — including Refineries, Chemical Plants, Power Plants, Steel Plants, Paper Mills, and various industrial process units where the transfer of fluids, gases, or steam demands precision and reliability. Driven by innovation, backed by quality, and trusted by industries worldwide — we continue to deliver engineering excellence that performs under pressure.
